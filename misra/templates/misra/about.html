About miSRA

miSRA collects public small-RNA sequencing experiments from the NCBI Sequence Read Archive and processes them all in the same way with sRNAbench: read preprocessing, miRNA and tRNA profiling against miRBase, MirGeneDB and PmiREN, and mapping to the genome and to bacterial and viral sequences. Sample metadata are collected from the submissions and harmonised.

miSRA is developed by the bioinformatics group of the University of Granada.
