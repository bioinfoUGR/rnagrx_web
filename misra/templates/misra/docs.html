How to find and select experiments

Three ways in

  • Browse shows all experiments in a table. The filters on the left narrow the table down.
  • The Studies tab shows the same filtered experiments grouped by study. Tick studies and use “Show experiments of ticked studies”, or add their matching experiments to the cart directly.
  • By taxonomy lets you walk from kingdom down to organism and open all experiments of a taxon.

Filters

  • Ticking several values in one filter finds experiments with any of them (OR). Values in different filters must all apply (AND).
  • The number next to each value is how many experiments you get if you tick it, given your other filters.
  • “not annotated” finds experiments where the value is unknown.
  • The search box looks for all words (at least 3 letters, prefixes allowed) in the experiment's metadata, its study title and abstract, and in accessions (e.g. SRP103227).
  • Every filtered view has its own address: bookmark it or send the link to a colleague.

The cart

  • Tick rows and use “Add ticked to cart”, or “Add all … matching to cart” for everything the filters match, across all pages. The cart holds up to {{ cart_limit|intcomma }} experiments.
  • The cart is linked to your browser and kept for {{ cart_days }} days after its last change. No account is needed.
  • On the cart page you can filter the cart, remove experiments, or keep only the matching ones.
  • “Save as selection” freezes the cart under a permanent link that you can share, cite or open later.
  • Downloads of reads and expression values, and new analyses with sRNAbench, will be added to the cart page.
