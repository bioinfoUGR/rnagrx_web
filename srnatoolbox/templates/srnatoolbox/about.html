About sRNAtoolbox

sRNAtoolbox is developed by the bioinformatics group of the University of Granada. The analyses run on our servers with the same programs that you can install yourself (see the code repository).

No account is needed. Every job has a private link, which is the only way to reach it: bookmark it to come back later. Uploaded files and results are deleted after {{ max_age_days }} days.
